How the books are proved each month. Bank movement per account must equal the statement to the fils; the ledger wallet balance must equal the exchange balance; salary expense must equal the payroll sheet; USD sent to each Egypt office must equal USD recorded as received in that office’s transfers log. The month locks on the 5th and is closed on the 6th–7th.
Products. WeMasterTrade figures cover the Dubai + Alexandria offices and WMT MENA revenue and payouts only — WeGolden money is fully excluded. WeGolden has its own expenses and operation profit, and no revenue yet (build phase).
Revenue sources. HubSpot “Total MENA Revenue” (unsummarized export, closed-won WMT deals by create date) for 14 MENA countries, plus the internal CRM deal export (crm.wemastertrade.com, cut 11-Aug-2026) as the authority for Syria and Iran — 16 MENA countries in total. Incomplete Checkout deals are excluded; HubSpot Syria rows were replaced by the CRM figures.
Payout sources. HubSpot “MENA Trader Payout” and “MENA IP Payout” (approval amount to withdraw, by ticket create date). Operation profit = revenue − payouts − costs − marketing. $9,166 of payouts dated before Aug 2025 fall outside the period and are excluded; one Aug ’25 Iraq trader ticket has no amount and is skipped.
Marketing. Facebook and Google paid-ads spend attributed to MENA, from the internal Paid Ads report. It is paid centrally by HQ, so it never passes through the Dubai or Alexandria accounts — it is deliberately absent from the expense tabs and from the bank reconciliation, and appears only as its own line here. Tracked from March 2026, figures in USD as reported at source (the Google account bills in CAD and the report converts it; no further conversion is applied here). Facebook / Google by month: Mar $6,471 / $15,866 · Apr $8,659 / $16,635 · May $12,929 / $15,751 · Jun $14,920 / $11,419 · Jul $21,450 / $18,673. Paused campaigns are included. Months before March show “—”. MENA attribution in the source report is campaign-name based and flagged there as partial coverage, so the figure is more likely to understate than overstate.
FX. 1 USD = 3.6725 AED (peg) · Egypt rows up to Feb 2026: Alexandria EGP at 52.93, WeGolden EGP at 45.11 per report. From Mar 2026 every Egypt row is booked in USD at the actual rate of the transfer that funded it (EGP amount and rate in the notes).
Markets. Revenue, customer payouts and paid-ads are split by the deal's country: Pakistan, Ethiopia and Nigeria each own their country; MENA is every other country. Office costs count toward MENA only (Dubai + Alexandria) until an office is assigned to another market. Expenses are shown per office, not per market.
Method. Money in = income + funding transfers received; money out = expenses. Internal transfers between own accounts are excluded from totals and charts (still visible in the source files). WeGolden setup costs (undated) sit in Feb 2026.
