How the books are proved each month. Bank movement per account must equal the statement to the fils; the ledger wallet balance must equal the exchange balance; salary expense must equal the payroll sheet; USD sent to each Egypt office must equal USD recorded as received in that office’s transfers log. The month locks on the 5th and is closed on the 6th–7th.
Products: WeMasterTrade figures cover the Dubai + Alexandria offices and WMT MENA revenue/payouts only — WeGolden money is fully excluded. The WeGolden section has its own expenses and operation profit (no revenue yet — build phase).
Revenue source: HubSpot custom report "Total MENA Revenue" (unsummarized export, closed-won WMT deals by create date) — 15 MENA countries, USD.
Payout sources: HubSpot reports "MENA Trader Payout" and "MENA IP Payout" (approval amount to withdraw, by ticket create date, USD). Operation profit = revenue − payouts − costs. $9,166 of payouts dated before Aug 2025 fall outside the dashboard period and are excluded; one Aug '25 Iraq trader ticket has no amount in HubSpot and is skipped.
FX assumptions: 1 USD = 3.6725 AED (peg) · Alexandria EGP at actual transfer rate 52.93 EGP/USD · WeGolden EGP at 45.11 EGP/USD (per report).
Method: "Money in" = income + funding transfers received; "Money out" = expenses. Internal transfers between own accounts are excluded from totals and charts (still visible in the source files). WeGolden setup costs (undated items) are assigned to Feb 2026.
Source files: listed with links in the “Audit trail” panel above.
